## Difficulty

When the game starts, choose one of three difficulty modes:

| Difficulty | Quiz questions | Knowledge lost for a wrong answer |
|---|---:|---:|
| Easy | 2 | 3 |
| Medium | 3 | 5 |
| Hard | 5 | 10 |
| One Life | 7 | 100 |

---

Higher difficulties ask more questions and remove more knowledge when an
answer is wrong. Correct answers still reward knowledge, while wrong answers
also increase stress.
